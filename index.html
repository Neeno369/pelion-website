Five disciplines

Train, fight, flow, breathe, recover

Most people pay a gym, a dojo, a yoga studio and a recovery spa separately. At The Pelion it is one key and one monthly price.

Racks, platforms, dumbbells to the heavy end and plate-loaded machines. Coached by the owner, a lifter for 24 years.
