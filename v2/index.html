Questions

Before you reserve.

Is the $50 deposit really refundable?

Yes. If the facility does not meet your complete expectations when it opens, the full deposit is returned.

How long does the founding rate last?

VIP Founders pay $159 a month on a 12-month contract. The standard rate at launch is $199 a month.

What is included in the membership?

Every tier includes unlimited, all-inclusive access to kickboxing and cardio kickboxing classes, the weight room for strength and conditioning, yoga classes, the breathwork studio and the recovery spa with sauna and cold plunge.

When do monthly payments start?

Only when the doors open. Your deposit holds the spot until then.

Do I need experience in martial arts or yoga?

No. Classes are coached for every level, from your first session to competition prep.

Will there be family or kids memberships?

Yes. Once The Pelion opens, family packages and kids memberships will be available at a discounted rate. Everyone on the list hears the details first.

Is there a sign-up fee?

Not for founders. Open Door memberships at launch have a $49 sign-up fee.
